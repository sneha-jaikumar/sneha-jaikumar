## 🤖 Featured work

### [Phone Video to Humanoid Motion](https://sneha-jaikumar.github.io/projects/video-to-robot-pipeline/) · AWS Physical AI
A one-command pipeline from handheld phone video to motion on a physical Unitree G1 (29-DOF), with no mocap suit or VR headset.
`3D pose estimation (GEM-X)` → `retargeting to G1 inverse kinematics (SOMA)` → `MuJoCo trajectory conversion` → `sim-to-sim validation` → `whole-body control (NVIDIA SONIC)` on the real robot

### [FurnishAI](https://sneha-jaikumar.github.io/projects/furnishai/) · AWS · 🏆 People's Choice Award, Amazon Global AI Solutions Expo
Turns a single room photo into a photorealistic furnished design with real, purchasable products. Scene understanding with **SAM** and **Depth Anything V2**, layout planning with a vision-language model, vector search over a product catalog, and depth-aware **Stable Diffusion inpainting**, one piece of furniture at a time.

### [Neural Signal Processing Pipeline](https://github.com/SNF-Center-for-Precision-Psychiarty/Electrophysiology-analysis-for-Intrinsic-properties) · Columbia
A from-scratch pipeline that automates hours of manual analysis of human neuron recordings: artifact rejection, spike detection, and 43+ features extracted from million-sample time-series data.

### [Text-to-Image Diffusion Model (GLIDE-inspired)](https://docs.google.com/document/d/16de9RqK3y0rQ6_g4TJtvDnsirfZYEEIxP0sifBG_BLg/edit?usp=sharing)
A scaled-down text-conditional diffusion model that generates images from written prompts, built from the ground up.
